Call Check

A scorecard that ranks every office on six rates: answer rate, business hours, after hours, new callers answered, missed callers reached and called back. Plus the monthly audit: fourteen yes/no behaviours per answered call, one focus per office, coaching lines and the recording.

Chart Check & Desk Check

Chart Check is the clinical and billing check on each walkout: doctor's notes, codes, consent. Desk Check is the front desk check after each visit: balances, insurance, next appointment, documents. An item is open until the office manager adds a comment.

Office managers

Pick a queue and a date, review each item, then acknowledge it. Not done, seen. Pending, acknowledged and total on one line. It is the smallest habit that keeps nothing from going quiet for three weeks.

Built for patient data

Boring where it should be boring.

Huddle was built for a dental group that lost answers when one saved copy of a shared file was written over another. Every rule below exists because something once went wrong without it.

Roles the server enforces

Owner, admin, central team, office manager, front desk. Hiding a button is never the only protection. Every refused request is logged.

Append-only change ledger

Every save, sync, block, export, sign-in and approval is written first to a ledger that is never pruned. You can always answer "who changed this, and when?"

Nothing can be wiped

A snapshot before every write. Syncs block on empty data or a 20%+ row drop. Documents keep their previous copy. Snapshots are kept 400 days, with a nightly copy to your drive.

HIPAA-aligned by design

Minimum-necessary access per role and per practice, named accounts, session limits, lockout after repeated bad passwords, and a Business Associate Agreement with every customer.

Two people approve the month

Monthly PTAR and insurance AR files are checked for empty rows, missing IDs, duplicates and wrong practices, kept exactly as sent, and approved by a second person before they reach a queue.

Read-only where it matters

Finance never connects to your bookkeeping or your bank. Call Check never connects to your phone system. Data arrives as checked packages an owner or admin loads by hand.

Questions

Asked at almost every demo.

Does Huddle replace our practice management system?

No. Eaglesoft, Dentrix or Open Dental stay the system of record for the chart and the schedule. Huddle takes the reports those systems already produce (PTAR, insurance AR, walkout audits) and turns them into queues with owners, notes and dates.

How long does it take to go live?

Under two weeks for most groups. Week one: we load your practices, roles and the wiki, and connect the monthly uploads. Week two: office managers work their real queues alongside us. There is no data migration because Huddle reads what you already export.

What can an office manager see?

Their own practice only: its queues, Call Check, Scoreboard and the wiki answers for their region. They comment, set status, complete the Daily Check-in, and export CSV. They cannot see other practices, Uploads or Pulse; those show as locked. The server enforces this on every request, and rows for other practices are never sent to their browser.

What is the central team, and do we need one?

The central team is the group's back office: the people who run audits, work insurance AR and route items to each practice. If yours is in-house, outsourced, or just two people, Huddle gives them a place to route work and a scoreboard to see it answered. Roles are owner, admin, central team, office manager and front desk.

Is Pulse really owners-only?

Yes. Pulse (Finance, HR, Payroll) opens only for accounts with the owner role. Admins who are not owners are refused by the server, and each refusal is logged. Everyone else sees a lock in the sidebar; the tab does not open.

How is pricing structured?

Per practice, per month, with the central team and owner seats included. Groups of ten or more practices get volume pricing. We quote after a 20-minute call where we look at your current reports together.

Where does our data live?

In the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest, with a nightly copy to a drive you own. We sign a Business Associate Agreement with every customer before any patient data is loaded.

Can we start with one or two practices?

Yes. Most groups start with the two offices where AR follow-up hurts most, then add the rest once the office managers ask for it. They usually do within the first month.
