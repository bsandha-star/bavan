    01 · Queues that work themselves

    Insurance AR and patient balances, resolved while you sleep.

    Huddle reads the aging report straight from your practice management system. For every unpaid, underpaid or denied claim it checks the payer portal, pulls the EOB, finds the missing attachment or the wrong code, corrects it and resubmits. For every patient balance it sends the statement, offers a payment plan or financing by text, and posts what comes back. What's left is a short list that needs a human, each with the fix already drafted.

    • Denials worked with the payer's reason and the correction, not a to-do
    • Patient balances offered a plan, financing or a two-tap payment link
    • Pre-auths submitted with the x-rays and narrative attached the first time
    • Every action written back to your PMS and the ledger under a name

Each office gets one focus a month and a coaching line on the calls that matter. The leaderboard makes it a game your front desks want to win.

    Built for patient data

    Boring where it should be boring.

    AI that touches claims, balances and payroll has to be trustworthy before it is clever. Every rule below is enforced on the server, not in a checkbox.

    Roles the server enforces

    Owner, admin, central team, office manager, front desk. Each sees only their practices. Pulse opens for owners alone, and every refused request is logged.

    Every AI action is ledgered

    Each claim worked, text sent, transaction categorized and roster change is written to an append-only ledger with what was done, why, and who approved it. You can always answer "who changed this?"

    A human in the loop where it counts

    Money leaving, a patient conversation, a warning letter, a payroll correction: the AI drafts, a person approves. Anything the AI is unsure about goes to our operations team first.

    HIPAA-aligned by design

    Minimum-necessary access per role and per practice, named accounts, Google sign-in, session limits, encryption in transit and at rest, and a Business Associate Agreement with every customer.

    Nothing can be wiped

    A snapshot before every write. Syncs block on empty data or a 20%+ row drop. Documents keep every version. Snapshots are kept 400 days with a nightly copy to a drive you own.

    Your systems stay the record

    Huddle writes back to your PMS, your books and your payroll provider. It never becomes the only copy of anything. Leave any time and take everything with you.

    Questions

    Asked at almost every demo.

    Does Huddle replace our practice management system?

    No. Eaglesoft, Dentrix, Open Dental, CareStack or whichever you run stays the system of record for the chart, the schedule and the ledger. Huddle connects behind it, works the queues, and writes the results back. Your team keeps the tools they know.

    What does "press the button" really mean? What does the AI do and what do people do?

    The AI does the repetitive work: reading aging reports, checking payer portals, pulling EOBs, correcting codes, resubmitting, texting patients, categorizing transactions, building rosters, scoring calls. Our operations team, people who have run dental front desks and closed dental books, reviews anything the AI isn't confident about. Your office manager approves the few items that need judgment about a patient or a payer. Money never leaves and no warning letter goes out without a human approval.

    How long does it take to go live?

    About two weeks for most groups. Week one: we connect your PMS, phones, financing and bank feeds, load your standards into the Dental Wiki, and set roles. Week two: office managers work their real queues alongside our team. There is no data migration because Huddle reads what your systems already hold.

    What can an office manager see?

    Their own practice: its queues, Blind Spots, Call Check, the leaderboard, the wiki and the daily huddle. They approve, comment and resolve. They cannot see other practices or Pulse. The server enforces this on every request; rows for other practices are never sent to their browser.

    Is Pulse really owners-only?

    Yes. Bottom Line, Roster, On Time, Staff File and Payday open only for accounts with the owner role. Admins who are not owners are refused by the server, and each refusal is logged. Everyone else sees a lock in the sidebar.

    How is pricing structured?

    A platform fee per practice, then a share of the work actually done: AR collected, balances recovered, books closed, payroll periods run. Volume pricing from ten practices. No long contracts. We quote after a 20-minute call where we look at your numbers together.

    Where does our data live, and can we leave?

    In the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest, with a nightly copy to a drive you own. We sign a Business Associate Agreement before any patient data is loaded. Because Huddle writes back to your own systems, leaving means turning it off. Everything stays where it already was.

    Can we start with Workspace only, or Pulse only?

    Yes. Many groups start with Workspace at two offices where AR hurts most, then add Pulse when the owner is ready to hand off the books. Others start with Pulse because the month-end is the pain. Each product stands alone; together they share one set of numbers.
