Workspace runs the office.

For office managers and front desks. Seven chores that used to be your job, each now a queue, a caller, a coach or a brain, connected to the systems behind the desk and learning your practice.

  1. Queues that work themselves

    Insurance AR, patient balances and pre-auths, read from your practice management system and worked overnight. Huddle checks the payer portal, pulls the EOB, fixes the attachment or code and resubmits; pre-auths go out with x-rays and narrative the first time; patients get a statement, a plan, financing or a two-tap pay link. Every action is written back to your PMS and the ledger under a name.

    Each evening a warm voice AI calls the patients due for tomorrow’s gaps, by name, inside your hours and attempt limits, offers two real openings and books one. Voicemail gets a “reply 1 or 2” text. Pain goes to a short visit, red flags and money to your manager, and “stop calling” is honored at once. Workspace only reads from the calling platform.

Asked at almost every demo.

Does Huddle replace our practice management system?

No. Eaglesoft, Dentrix, Open Dental, CareStack or whichever you run stays the system of record for the chart, the schedule and the ledger. Huddle connects behind it, works the queues, and writes the results back. Your team keeps the tools they know.

What does the AI do, and what do people do?

The AI does the repetitive work: reading aging reports, checking payer portals, pulling EOBs, correcting codes, resubmitting, texting patients, categorizing transactions, building rosters, scoring calls. Our operations team, people who have run dental front desks and closed dental books, reviews anything the AI isn’t confident about. Your office manager approves the few items that need judgment about a patient or a payer. Money never leaves and no warning letter goes out without a human approval.

How long does it take to go live?

About two weeks for most groups. Week one: we connect your PMS, phones, financing and bank feeds, load your standards into the Dental Wiki, and set roles. Week two: office managers work their real queues alongside our team. There is no data migration because Huddle reads what your systems already hold.

What can an office manager see?

Their own practice: its queues, recall calls, daily audits, phone coaching, the financing leaderboard, team training and the daily huddle. They approve, comment and resolve. They cannot see other practices or Pulse. The server enforces this on every request; rows for other practices are never sent to their browser.

Does the voice AI really call my patients?

Yes. Open Chair calls patients who are due for a check-up, in the hours and attempt limits you set, and only after your consent and disclosure rules are in place. It greets by name, says why it’s calling, offers two real openings and books the one they choose. It never discusses clinical findings, insurance details or balances; those go to your manager. Asked whether it is a person, it says honestly that it is the office’s automated assistant. If someone says stop, it stops. Every call summary and recording is in Workspace the next morning.

Is Pulse really owners-only?

Yes. The owner pages (your books, staff scheduling, attendance, HR records, payroll and your SEO and SEM) open only for accounts with the owner role. Admins who are not owners are refused by the server, and each refusal is logged. Everyone else sees a lock in the sidebar.

How is pricing structured?

A platform fee per practice, then a share of the work actually done: AR collected, balances recovered, books closed, payroll periods run. Volume pricing from ten practices. No long contracts. We quote after a 20-minute call where we look at your numbers together.

Where does our data live, and can we leave?

In the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest, with a nightly copy to a drive you own. We sign a Business Associate Agreement before any patient data is loaded. Because Huddle writes back to your own systems, leaving means turning it off. Everything stays where it already was.

Can we start with Workspace only, or Pulse only?

Yes. Many groups start with Workspace at two offices where AR hurts most, then add Pulse when the owner is ready to hand off the books. Others start with Pulse because the month-end is the pain. Each product stands alone; together they share one set of numbers.
